LunaSite is an interactive, zero-install web application that helps NASA mission planners, educators, and the public evaluate landing sites at the Moon's south pole. Using real NASA topographic data and orbital mechanics, LunaSite visualizes when the Sun and Earth are visible above actual crater rims rather than an artificial flat horizon. Users can instantly compare landing sites, find optimal mission dates, and verify continuous solar power generation alongside direct-to-Earth communications without complex desktop software.
